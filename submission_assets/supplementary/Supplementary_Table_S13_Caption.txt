Supplementary Table S13. Sensitivity of the Stage II ranking to the subjective-objective weight blend. The parameter beta is the share of each final criterion weight taken from the aggregated expert ratings, the remainder coming from the CRITIC contrast weights, so beta = 0 applies the CRITIC weights alone and beta = 1 the expert weights alone; the manuscript holds beta at 0.5. It is distinct from the Stage III confidence levels alpha_j. Cells give the suitability score Q01 and the rank in parentheses. The ordering is S5 > S4 > S2 > S3 > S1 at every value tested, so no rank reversal occurs anywhere on the interval. The lead of the first site over the second narrows monotonically from 0.044 at beta = 0 to 0.036 at beta = 1, that is, the separation of S5 is widest when the weights come entirely from the measured data and narrowest when they come entirely from the panel. The last column reports the share of the final weight carried by the four measured criteria K1-K4. This check varies the weight blend; the method variants of Table S5 vary the data mapping, the CRITIC normalisation and the use of fuzzy arithmetic.
