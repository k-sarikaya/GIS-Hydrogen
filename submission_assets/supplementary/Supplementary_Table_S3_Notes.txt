**Cross-check with an independent review.** Zun & McLellan [38] reviewed 32 studies with 248 unique criteria. By category these were economic 33, environmental 31, technical 29, social 14 and risk & safety 9. Weighting the criteria by how often they occur in the literature gives resource potential ≈34%, H₂ demand 10%, DEM 10% and distance to main road 9%. Our ordering matches this. Both reviews also find that **risk/safety and social criteria are under-represented** relative to their practical importance [38]. Mirsane et al. [39] and Pineda Serna et al. [40] likewise list resource, slope, grid, transport, demand and land-use as the most frequent criteria, with public acceptance as a recurring soft criterion.

**Patterns relevant to this study**

1. Physical and spatial criteria are almost always measured (Q). Expert-judged decision matrices (J) are used mainly for criteria without data, such as social acceptance, policy, risk and, in fuzzy studies, cost.
2. Recent fuzzy hydrogen-siting studies [9, 10] use a **hybrid** matrix: data for measurable criteria and linguistic expert judgement for the rest.
3. No hydrogen-*production* siting study in the sample uses geological storage as a ranking criterion. It appears only in UHS siting [21, 22, 27].
4. Water *stress* (as opposed to distance to water) is an emerging criterion [5, 7, 13, 14], strongly motivated by Tonelli et al. [41].

--- Criteria considered and not retained ---
| Criterion | Reason |
|---|---|
| C1a wind CF, C1b GHI (as separate criteria) | Causally embedded in T1. Keeping them with T1 double counts. Retain as reported descriptors and as a robustness variant ("CF + GHI instead of LCOH") |
| C8 CO₂ abatement | An outcome of Stage III allocation, not a site attribute. Excluded as an optimisation output in [38]. Report it as a Stage III result |
| C10 incentive-zone rating | See below |
| Slope, elevation, contiguous area, cropland | Fully handled as Stage I hard constraints; little residual variance among the 5 candidates |
| Distance to settlements | Covered by the Stage I built-up buffer and by S1. Direction is ambiguous in the literature (labour access vs. nuisance) |
| Road access, port access | Low discrimination at this scale. Captured in E1 and E2 |

**Why C10 is dropped:**
- Its legal basis, Decree 2012/3305, was repealed by Decree 9903 (Art. 34) [G1].
- Decree 9903 keeps six regions (Annex 2). The Ministry's presentation lists Denizli 2, Kırıkkale 3, Malatya 4, Tunceli 5 and Muş 6 [G2]; the Official Gazette text of Annex 2 was not consulted directly.
- Temporary Art. 3 grants Region-6 supports to districts affected by the 6 February 2023 earthquakes for applications until 31/12/2026. Whether this applies to the candidate districts was not established.
- Arts. 9–10 withhold some supports (tax reduction, interest support) from electricity-generation investments, so zone rank is not a monotone proxy for GH₂ attractiveness.
- The zone is therefore time-varying and weakly relevant to the siting decision.
