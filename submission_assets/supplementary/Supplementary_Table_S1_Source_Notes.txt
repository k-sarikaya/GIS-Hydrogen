# Demand node inventory

Industrial hydrogen consumers in Türkiye, compiled on 16 October 2026 and prepared independently of the Stage I candidate sites.

## Selection rule (independent of the candidate sites)

| Rule | Definition | Nodes |
|---|---|---|
| `rule_baseline` | Plants operating today that use or produce hydrogen on site (refineries and on-site ammonia synthesis) with an estimated demand of at least 1,000 t H2/yr | 7 |
| `rule_2040` | `rule_baseline` plus integrated steel plants that have announced a transition to H2-DRI in their roadmaps (Erdemir, Isdemir) | 9 |

Plants considered and not included:

- **Fertiliser plants using imported ammonia:** Toros, BAGFAS, Gubretas, Ege Gubre.
- **Petkim:** hydrogen arises as a by-product; the quantity is not published.
- **Kardemir:** no H2 or DRI project.
- **Tosyali:** no verified hydrogen demand in Türkiye.
- **Habas:** below 1,000 t/yr.
- **HySouthMarmara:** still at project stage.

## Value types

- `size_status` and `h2_value_type`: **P** = published value, **E** = estimate.
- No company publishes its hydrogen consumption in tonnes; refinery and ammonia demands are estimated from capacity multiplied by a conversion factor.
- `h2_onpurpose_t_yr` is the share supplied by dedicated hydrogen units (steam methane reforming) at refineries. This is the part that electrolytic hydrogen can substitute directly; the remainder of total consumption is by-product hydrogen from reformers and crackers.

## Conversion factors

| Factor | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ammonia | 0.1776 t H2 per t NH3 (stoichiometric: 3 x 2.016 / 2 x 17.031) | - |
| Refinery, total H2 | about 9.6 kg H2 per t of crude (range 9.0-10.3) | CATF 2025: EU refinery hydrogen use 4,205 kt/yr against an EU capacity of 11.7 Mbbl/d, assuming 7.33 bbl/t and 75% utilisation (S24) |
| Refinery, on-purpose share | about 5.5 kg H2 per t of crude, roughly 60% of the total | CATF 2025 (S24) |
| H2-DRI | 47-57 kg H2 per t of DRI | Vogl et al. 2018, J. Clean. Prod. 203:736, doi:10.1016/j.jclepro.2018.08.279; Rechberger et al. 2020, steel research int., doi:10.1002/srin.202000110 |
| Volume to mass | 1 Nm3 H2 = 0.08988 kg | - |

**Limitation.** The EU average factors understate complex refineries (Izmit, STAR) and overstate the simple Batman refinery. The published hydrogen unit capacity of Izmit and STAR, about 160,000 Nm3/h each, corresponds to roughly 126 kt/yr at nameplate and exceeds the factor-based estimate.

## Verification status

- IGSAS, 396,000 t NH3/yr: verified against the plant page at yildiz.com.
- Tupras capacities of 11.3, 11.9, 5.4 and 1.4 Mt/yr: verified against tupras.com.tr and the December 2024 investor presentation.
- Checks used the primary sources listed below and were completed on 16 October 2026. Other values follow the cited sources without independent verification; their evidence levels are recorded in Table S1.

## National context

- **Strategy targets.** Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources, Hydrogen Technologies Strategy and Roadmap (January 2023): electrolyser capacity of 2 GW by 2030, 5 GW by 2035 and 70 GW by 2053. No tonnage target for demand is set (S21).
- **National grey hydrogen base (estimate).** Refineries about 0.41 Mt, of which about 0.24 Mt is on-purpose, and ammonia about 0.08-0.14 Mt, giving roughly 0.5 Mt H2/yr in total. No official national figure was found.
- **Third-party projections.** EBRD and Advisian put low-carbon hydrogen demand at about 60 kt/yr in 2030 and about 725 kt/yr in 2040 (S22). SHURA 2022 gives 3.4 Mt/yr in 2050 (S23).

## Renewable share and delivery points used in Stage III

- The renewable (RFNBO) share differs by sector. Ammonia complexes use the RED III industry target: 33.6 % / 42 % / 60 % (Art. 22b reduced 2030, Art. 22a 2030, Art. 22a 2035). Refinery hydrogen used as an intermediate for transport fuels falls outside the industry target and counts towards the transport sub-target (at least 1 percentage point RFNBO in 2030, counted twice). Applied to Türkiye's 2024 transport final energy without aviation (1 124.7 PJ; TÜİK, March 2026), the sub-target corresponds to 46.9 kt H₂/yr (mode), 93.7 kt without the multiplier (high) and 37.5 kt (low, 0.8 × mode). As shares of the 237 kt refinery on-purpose demand: 0.158 / 0.198 / 0.395. Derivation: Supplementary Table S2, Note N11.
- Modal renewable demand: ammonia 58.3 kt + refineries 46.9 kt = 105.2 kt/yr (possibility range 84.2–177.1 kt/yr). Applying the industry share to every facility (upper-bound scenario) gives 157.9 kt/yr.
- Facilities less than 2 km apart share one delivery point in Stage III: İzmir (Aliağa) and STAR refineries (Aliağa hub); İzmit refinery and İGSAŞ (Körfez hub). Arc lengths to a hub are the mean of the members' road distances. The import penalty of a hub weights each member's sector parity by its demand range.
