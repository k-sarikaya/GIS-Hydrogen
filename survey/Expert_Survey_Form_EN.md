## Introduction

This form concerns five candidate green-hydrogen production sites. They were identified for the whole of Türkiye with a geographic information system (GIS) and literature-based exclusion criteria: elevation, protected areas, settlements, airports, water bodies, land cover, slope, wind and solar resource, and water and grid access.

The sites are compared on seven criteria:

- **Four criteria are computed from open data** by the research team: grid proximity, water stress, ecological sensitivity and seismic hazard.
- **Three criteria can only be judged by experts:** local social acceptance; permitting and regulatory readiness; and the industrial-logistics ecosystem.

You are asked to:

1. **Part C:** rate the general importance of the seven criteria;
2. **Part D:** rate the five sites on the three expert-judged criteria.

Your judgements will be modelled with spherical fuzzy sets. These represent the degree of positive opinion (μ), negative opinion (ν) and hesitancy (π) independently.

**Privacy and consent.** Participation is voluntary. Responses are used only in aggregated, anonymised form and for academic purposes. Personal data are protected under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). Name and e-mail are optional.
